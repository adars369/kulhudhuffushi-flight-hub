When it blows

HUB · MEAN WIND m/s · MONTH × HOUR · 2021–2025

The southwest monsoon (May–Oct) runs 6–7 m/s round the clock with a mid-afternoon bump; January–April sits near 3–4 m/s. Fly the dry season in the morning.

Where from

January–April gives 92–99% flyable hours at every pad. May is the worst month on the network: about 2 hours in 3. Estimate treats gust and rain hours as independent.

Range against the spec

ROUND TRIP km

The tender's 80 km per charge covers only two post offices out and back. The loop plan caps every pack at 57.1 km with a swap at Hoarafushi, Makunudhoo and Funadhoo.

Airports on the route

RUNWAYS FROM OPENSTREETMAP · APPROACH = ICAO ANNEX 14 GEOMETRY

Hoarafushi and Funadhoo post offices sit about half a kilometre from a runway threshold, just beside the approach path. Two legs cross an approach: hub → Nolhivaranfaru (VRBK runway 30) and Hoarafushi → hub (VRAH runway 15). Those crossings need a slot or a corridor agreed with the airport. Approach surfaces are drawn to ICAO Annex 14 proportions for planning only; the published MCAA airspace decides.

Rules of the air for this pilot

  • CorridorLetter of agreement with each airport: fixed corridor, max 120 m, tower or AFIS contact before entering the 5 km buffer.
  • SlotsNo launches or landings at VRBK, VRAH, VRCF within 15 minutes of a scheduled movement.
  • Side entryApproach Hoarafushi and Funadhoo pads at right angles to the runway, never along the extended centreline.
  • ADS-BADS-B In/Out plus satellite tracking on the drone, the combination the tender Q&A accepted.
